Job finder

Every 2 hours from 09:00 to 19:00 IST it reads Naukri's own search results for today (in a muted background tab, never applying to anything) and sends the jobs that fit to your site's Openings tab, as Naukri today. There are two lists, one per Naukri account: Backend (backend-first roles, checked against your SDE resume) and AI (AI-first roles, checked against your GenAI resume). Each keeps its own top 50 a day, and a job is on one list only.

To connect this browser, open your site's Job tracker here while you're signed in to it. The page hands this extension a token of its own, which can only send job finder openings. There's nothing to copy.

Paste a token instead

Your PC radar's RADAR_INGEST_TOKEN works too. This PowerShell line copies it from .radar\.env:

(Select-String -Path C:\PROJECTS\CodeNITW\.radar\.env -Pattern '^RADAR_INGEST_TOKEN=').Line.Split('=',2)[1] | Set-Clipboard
