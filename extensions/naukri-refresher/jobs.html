Job finder

Every day around 17:00 IST it reads Naukri's own search results, every page of every search, in a muted background tab, and never applies to anything. It works in hour-long stretches with 10-minute rests until every listing of the last 48 hours is read. A job it judged in an earlier run (the last 3 days) is skipped: no JD is opened twice and nothing is sent or rated twice. For every card from the last 48 hours that looks right (and isn't from a company on your low-pay list), it opens the job's page and reads the JD. It keeps the job only if enough of the technologies the JD asks for are yours. The best 400 it keeps per list go to your site's Openings tab, under ⭐ Naukri, last 48 hours, and each one is then rated 1–100 against your resume by an LLM on Groq. There are two lists, one per Naukri account: Backend (backend-first roles) and AI (AI-first roles, where GenAI work is a large share of the JD). A job is on one list only. Your site shows the top 100 per list that you haven't applied to or taken yet, highest rating first.

AI rating (Groq)

Checking…

Each job sent to your site is read by an LLM on Groq against your resume (the SDE one for the Backend list, the GenAI one for the AI list). The model only lists the JD's requirements and, for each, whether your resume shows it, quoting the resume. The rating is worked out here with fixed weights, and a requirement counts as met only when its quote is really in your resume, word for word. Paste as many keys as you have (up to 10), comma-separated: it uses every one, each in turn, and a key at its limit rests while the others work. The keys stay in this browser and go only to api.groq.com; your name and contact lines are never sent.
