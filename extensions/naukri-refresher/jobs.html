Job finder

Every 2 hours from 09:00 to 19:00 IST it reads Naukri's own search results for today, in a muted background tab, and never applies to anything. For every card that looks right, it opens the job's page and reads the JD. It keeps the job only if enough of the technologies the JD asks for are yours. The jobs it keeps go to your site's Openings tab, as Naukri today. There are two lists, one per Naukri account: Backend (backend-first roles) and AI (AI-first roles, where GenAI work is a large share of the JD). A job is on one list only. Each list aims for 100 a day.

Paste a token instead

Your PC radar's RADAR_INGEST_TOKEN works too. This PowerShell line copies it from .radar\.env:

(Select-String -Path C:\PROJECTS\CodeNITW\.radar\.env -Pattern '^RADAR_INGEST_TOKEN=').Line.Split('=',2)[1] | Set-Clipboard
