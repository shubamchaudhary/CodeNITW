# Lecture series plan: chapters 3–12

One story runs through every lecture: **FreshCart**, a grocery delivery company in Pune and Bengaluru, and the people who use what you build: **Meera** (runs support, 80 agents), **Arjun** (warehouse planner), the **COO**, **security** and **finance**. Each lecture opens on the problem the previous one left behind, builds the fix as something you can see move, names it only after it is understood (`term`), and ends on the next problem as a "Pause and think" plus the next chapter's title.

Style rules (from ch01–ch02 feedback):
- picture first, term second; plain words; one idea per beat;
- motion, not slides: things move, fill, split, spin, light up;
- tone on what matters (`key`, `ask`, `excite`, `aside`);
- understanding over coverage: 6–9 minutes, the notes hold the rest;
- every card sizes to its text: no fixed heights on text boxes, and `qa/check.html` must report no overflow before a chapter is done.
